Frequently asked questions

What is PrimeFlix?+

PrimeFlix is a place to discover and watch movies and series published in our library.

How do I create an account?+

Enter your email above or choose Sign In, then select Create an account. We will send a verification link to your email.

Where can I watch?+

Open PrimeFlix in a modern web browser on a computer, tablet, or phone.

How do I save a title?+

Sign in and use the plus button on a title to add it to My List.
